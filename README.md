## ✨ Features

### 🐾 Wild Evomon Radar & ESP
- **Live World Radar**: Scans and tracks all 280+ wild Evomons across the map in real-time.
- **Evomon ESP Visualizer**: Shows clean billboard tags with Evomon Name, Level, and distance in studs.
- **Auto Farm Wild Evomons**: Auto-teleports to eligible nearby wild Evomons to initiate battles continuously.
- **Fast Travel**:
  - `Teleport To Closest Evomon`
  - `Teleport To Highest Level Evomon` (Finds Lvl 165+ Starloop, Lvl 180 Thordlord, etc.)

### ⚔️ Auto Battle & Catch
- **Auto Battle Mode**: Automatically triggers and executes turn-based skill moves.
- **Smart Escape**: Auto-flees battles if the enemy level exceeds your configured safety threshold.
- **Manual Flee Button**: Single click escape button to exit any battle instantly.

- **Custom Code Input**: Easily test and redeem new promo codes.

- **Auto Claim Daily & Group Rewards**: One-click collection for daily streaks and group bonuses.

### 🗺️ World & Island Teleports
- Direct waypoints to all 10+ Islands (Starter, Misty Shore, Breeze Woods, Frost Peaks, Molten Core, Starfall Sanctuary, etc.).
- Fast access to S3 Trading Hall and Dungeons.

- Custom WalkSpeed (16 - 150) & JumpPower (50 - 250).
